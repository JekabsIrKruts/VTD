How to play

Build a team of frogs, protect the path, and survive all 10 waves. Enemies that reach the end deal damage to your HP.

1. Place your frogs

You start with 1,000 wings (ω). Choose a frog from the top of the screen, then drag it onto an open green space. Frogs cannot be placed on the path. They automatically attack enemies in range. Hover over a frog, or drag one to see its range.

2. Prepare for each wave

Before a wave, answer its question and plan your defense. Select Next Round to preview the enemy types and counts, then choose Start Wave when you are ready. You can place and upgrade frogs during a wave too.

3. Answer the questions

Choose an answer by clicking it or pressing 1, 2, or 3. Correct frog answers add 2,000 points. A wrong answer makes that wave 1.5× faster; clearing it earns a 1,000-point bonus. Frog questions can be skipped. After a skip, the next wave gets a harder fly question instead: it cannot be skipped, a correct answer adds 6,000 points, and a wrong answer makes that wave 2× faster with a 3,000-point clear bonus.

4. Earn and spend wings

Frogs earn wings when they defeat enemies. Spend wings to place frogs and buy upgrades: click a placed frog to view its next upgrade, price, and effects. Each frog can be upgraded through three tiers.

5. Protect your HP

You start with 1,000 HP, which carries between waves. Enemies deal different damage when they reach the end. If HP reaches zero, the game ends.

Mama Frog’s offering

Before wave 6, choose whether to trade HP for wings at the displayed rate, while keeping at least 1 HP. If you are at full HP, you can instead sacrifice all but 1 HP to bless the remaining flies, reducing their HP by 10%.

Score and finish

Your wave score starts at 10,000 and counts down after all enemies have entered. Clear the wave to add its remaining points and any quiz bonus to your total. Survive all 10 waves, then save your username and score to the leaderboard.

Pause any time

Use Pause to stop the action. Resume the game, return to the main menu, or exit from the pause menu.
